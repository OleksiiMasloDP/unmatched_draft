The Wayward Sisters: Unmatched hero guide

The Wayward Sisters is an Unmatched hero from Slings and Arrows. Elo rating 1439, ranked #49 of 74 heroes. Playstyle: Value / Combo. Complexity: 4.5/5.

Strengths

Unlike other 3-figure fighters who cluster to gang up on a target, the Sisters prefer spreading across separate zones of the map so the opponent has nowhere to hide — and on many maps they can reach without a movement boost.

Can hide a wounded Sister and heal her while fighting with a healthy one instead, making it very hard for the opponent to finish her off — damage they deal just gets erased over time.

On item-based maps, having three bodies simply lets you claim and control more items than most fighters.

Weaknesses

Cauldron management is fiddly — most cards are low printed value, and letting too many cards pile up in the cauldron without spending them on a spell wastes resources.

Several of her strongest cards (the 0-value zone-count attack/defense in particular) are printed at very low base value, making them extremely vulnerable to strong cancel/ignore-value effects.

Tempting to overuse the extra-action spell, but extra actions cost extra resources and don't always bring you closer to winning — especially against defense-heavy, high-healing, or high-draw opponents.

General tips

Spread your three Sisters across different zones rather than clustering them together.

Try to have at least one or two of your key scaling attack/defense cards in your opening hand — realizing their potential is central to the character.

Only reach for the extra-action spell when you have a strong follow-up combo or you're facing a low-defense opponent — otherwise it usually isn't worth the resources.

Don't let cards pile up unspent in the cauldron; look to cash them in on a spell rather than let the resource go to waste.

This is not a fatigue/attrition character — she doesn't have the tools to grind out a long war of attrition, so look to close games out decisively rather than stall.
