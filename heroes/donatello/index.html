Donatello: Unmatched hero guide

Donatello is an Unmatched hero from Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. Elo rating 1742, ranked #22 of 74 heroes. Playstyle: Combo / Tempo / Resource Engine. Complexity: 5/5.

Strengths

Strong combo-based win condition with multiple attack chains in one turn

High tempo through gaining extra actions from schemes and Metalhead cards

Powerful recursion engine allowing reuse of key attacks and defenses

Flexible combo paths: early pressure, surprise burst, or full setup finisher

Weaknesses

Relies heavily on combo timing — wrong moment can lose the game

Resource intensive: requires setup in discard and hand

Can run out of cards during early aggressive combos

Struggles if opponent avoids engagement or constantly defends

General tips

Plan your combo in advance — track damage and available actions

Keep at least one wagon for finishing

Use recursion cards to repeat your strongest attacks

Do not rush early combo unless necessary

Most games are won through a well-prepared full combo

Matchup tips

Daredevil: Daredevil's only real chance is to keep his hand low, blind boost and constantly attack Donatello.

Do not let him play a passive game — if he cannot find enough defense, catch him with your combo and finish the game.

Donatello's many Feints that cancel Daredevil's effects make his normal gameplan very unreliable.

Daredevil also loses the deck race and the health race, so he cannot afford to play for exhaustion.

Achilles: The matchup is played without Donatello's map because Achilles also wants a small map.

Achilles struggles on an open map, especially once Donatello kills Patroclus.

After Patroclus dies, use your mobility to run away from Achilles and make him chase you.

Your three Feints are especially valuable against Achilles' Spears, which he can otherwise recycle.

Active Inventions give Donatello solid defenses, making the matchup difficult for Achilles.

Leshen: Ignore Leshen for a long time and focus on attacking the Wolves.

Use your Returns around the Wolves and pressure them with the carts.

Build four Schemes in the discard, keep a cart and a Return available, then send in Metalhead and launch the combo.

A five-attack combo is usually enough to win the game or leave Leshen dead on the following turn.

Leshen can Vanish and throw a couple of strong attacks at you, but it is usually not enough to survive the combo.

T-Rex: This is an even matchup and essentially a damage race.

Donatello can close distance and draw cards, so he can actively keep up with T-Rex.

The matchup is decided by who assembles their winning combo first.

Play actively and look for your combo window rather than trying to win through a slow game.

Doctor Strange: This is close to an even matchup on Donatello's map.

On Strange's own map, especially Center 5 and other ranged maps, Strange has the advantage.

Use the red Invention against Wong, because Strange can use his Mists to discard the active Invention.

Ideally, kill Wong first and use Returns to bring carts back.

Then pressure Strange with carts and transition into your combo.

Strange may either blind boost heavily or maneuver away, but Metalhead is difficult for him to kill.

His healing is less effective against Donatello because Donatello's defenses are 4.

Bigfoot: Bigfoot looks difficult because his defenses appear to be mostly 4s, but most of them are actually Feints.

Only six of his defenses are 4s, while the rest are Feints that Donatello is happy to attack into.

Do not be afraid of his Feints — even when he cancels your effect, you still deal two damage.

The goal is to exhaust his real defenses and keep attacking him with your small attacks and combo pieces.

Yennenga: Her plan is to outlast you by redirecting damage into her archers and then getting it back from them.

Your main goal is to bait out all of Enenra’s defense cards with value 4 — your combo turn becomes much stronger once they are gone

Avoid overcommitting into her proactive turns — she wants you to walk into her pressure

Play patiently and build your combo while minimizing unnecessary damage taken

Metalhead can be ignored by Yennenga, so use it mainly for value and combo setup, not pressure

Try to keep your hand size healthy — a larger hand makes your combo turn much stronger

Be careful around her archers — avoid giving free value by feeding them with carts without purpose

Moon Knight: He'll pressure and defend actively.

Your goal is to catch him in Moon Knight form, play your carts, and spin up your own combo.

Golden Bat: Don't let him pick the map — he'll simply outrun you.

On a smaller map he dies to a combo, since he doesn't like being hit often.

Eredin: You can combo him out very well while his sidekicks are still alive.

Play your carts before he uses his scheme to ignore auto-damage.

If he's already used it, either run away or pressure him actively enough that he can't attack.

John Henry: A very tight damage race — find your red invention as fast as possible.

That said, he doesn't have that many good defenses.

Alice: Try to catch her mirror card with a cancel so she can't escape and heal.

Sinbad: He has a lot of free escapes, but you still need to keep hitting him — eventually he won't be able to keep up.

King Arthur: He spends too many cards as boost into your defenses, leaving him short on damage.

Bruce Lee: He won't have enough damage for you.

Your Feint works great here, cancelling his extra effects.

Robin Hood: He dies quickly since all his defenses cap out at 3.

Be careful of his 8 auto-damage, but once you have your invention in defense, his other attacks aren't nearly as scary.

Raptors: You usually have enough defenses for their attacks.

Play your cancels back to deny them extra actions.

Pressure their sidekick and they die to a combo.

They can win a long fatigue game on a large map, though.

Winter Soldier: Keep him under constant pressure — he shouldn't survive a combo turn.

Remember he can heal if he wins a fight with his 3.

Luke Cage: You can push a decent amount of auto-damage through him.

If you kill his sidekick quickly, you can even outrun him.

Black Widow: On her own map she can try to outrun you and win on fatigue.

Otherwise, finish her off with a combo turn.

Bullseye: His defenses are too low — he should die to a combo turn unless he manages to run.

The Genie: Kill him faster than he kills you — Donatello has enough draw, strong attacks, and good effects that he has no answer to.

Nikola Tesla: He dislikes pressure, so give it to him.

He can discard your good cards, which is unpleasant.

Don't pick a map with items.

Don't let him sit on two coils.

Tomoe Gozen: This is a Metalhead-vs-Tomoe matchup — all her cards work worse against him.

If you're playing your red invention actively, don't forget to keep a Feint ready in attack too, since she can play her demon.

The Wayward Sisters: They lack the damage — you'll kill them faster.

Philippa: Bring her health down to 6, play 2 carts and any attack, and she dies.

Chupacabra: He doesn't like being hit often, and Donatello is good at exactly that.

Despite his repeatable 3-defense, it's still only a 3

Michelangelo: He can't pierce your defense — he just runs towards you, and you run from him.

Wyatt Earp: Kill his sidekick and he won't have enough damage to win.

Rosie the Riveter: She has no good defenses while she has 2 or fewer upgrades — catch her in that window and land a combo turn before she can cancel it.

George Washington: He can discard a scheme for a combo turn, so don't hold a scheme in hand while his sidekicks are on the board.

Remove his spies first — that strips some of his defenses and hand discards.

He has a lot of good defenses.

Ghost Rider: Comes down to who kills who first.

On his own map this will be problematic for you.

Dr. Sattler: A lot of unpleasant effects during combat.

Her plan is to quickly kill Metalhead and then play her healing-focused game.

Black Panther: Everything depends on what he manages to steal — if it's your invention, it's over.

Otherwise he tries to pierce you with boosts but usually dies before that works.

Pandora: Depends on how her box lands.

Cancel her healing and pierce her hard.

Krang: He has a lot of strong effects and healing.

He doesn't like being pressured, so play into that.

He has a lot of good defenses — bait out his strong universal cards so he plays them defensively instead of attacking, leaving him short on damage.

Annie Christmas: The idea is to bring her down to 1-2 HP, then find a cart or auto-damage in defense so her own defense can't save her.

Shredder: A race for who lands their combo first.

Dracula: His plan is to kill Metalhead with auto-damage, then run and slowly chip away at you.

Once his health is low, he'll attack with his Wolf Form, and there's unfortunately not much you can do about it.

You need to land your combo on him in time.

Robert Muldoon: You need the right map.

He doesn't have that many defenses, so you need to combo him out.

Kill his sidekicks with your carts or with your +1 draw card to shuffle carts back in.

Use 'Shift Focus' to pull him toward you and set up a combo.

You can attack with your cancels so he can't retreat.

Sherlock Holmes: If you get lucky, you can try to combo him out.

Medusa: Too many discards and too much blocking from her sidekicks.

She can kill Metalhead with her 'Double Shot' combo.

Geralt of Rivia: Make sure to kill his sidekick first.

That said, his cards are generally better, so it'll still be a hard matchup.

Leonardo: If he uses his ability correctly and discards Donatello's cards, he simply outlasts him on fatigue.

Little Red Riding Hood: It won't be too hard for her to find damage against you, especially through auto-damage.

A long game favors her, so try to kill her faster than she kills you, at full speed.

Jill Trent: Too many defenses, too many effects.

Yennefer & Triss: You must pick the right map.

She doesn't have that many defenses — catch her before she discards your important cards.

Raphael: His plan is to run from you, occasionally attack (more often through his sidekick), build up a hand of defenses/universal cards, and then land 'Unbridled Rage' — and there's not much you can do about it.

Sun Wukong: Even if you manage to reach him, you won't be able to pierce through.

Blackbeard: Hard to play against because of his discards — you need to find your red invention very quickly to take his doubloons.

Elektra: You need to fight through her sidekicks, who won't be able to hit you thanks to your auto-damage in defense.

If you manage to kill her, make sure you're not holding a scheme for a combo, or she'll discard it.

Ciri: She has strong defenses while active, especially with discards — her plan is simply to outlast you, which she usually manages easily.

She-Hulk: Since you want to initiate combat, her ability works great against you.

You can't cancel her 9-value attack.

She can easily land her effects on Metalhead.

She discards cards in defense.

Your hope is that she runs low on defenses during your combo turn.

Harry Houdini: His cards are simply too good overall, plus controlled discards and free escapes into defense — landing a combo is extremely hard, and so is just surviving.
