Pandora: Unmatched hero guide

Pandora is an Unmatched hero from Battle of Legends, Volume Three. Elo rating 1580, ranked #36 of 74 heroes. Playstyle: Tempo / Value / Risk-Management / Control. Complexity: 4/5.

Strengths

Unique mechanic with Pandora’s Box creating constant value and decision pressure every turn

Strong synergy between ability and deck — effects amplify each other

Flexible playstyle: can play slow value, control tempo, or push aggression depending on flips

Access to additional value through sidekicks and box effects

Weaknesses

High randomness: outcomes depend heavily on box flips

Self-damage risk when pushing too many flips (feathers)

Requires strong decision-making on when to stop opening cards

Low HP on sidekicks makes them easy to remove

General tips

Do not greed flips — stopping early is often stronger than risking damage

Track feathers probability and manage risk accordingly

Use sidekicks actively but don’t overcommit them

Adapt your plan based on current box outcomes

Your strength is consistency over time, not explosive turns

Matchup tips

Leshen: Open the Box aggressively and use the accumulated value to overwhelm Leshen before it can stabilize.

After a couple of strong Box turns, use Zeus, Teleport and Hera to finish the fight.

Leshen simply cannot survive Pandora's repeated burst for long.

Raptors: Pandora has a strong advantage thanks to superior card draw, defensive retreats and useful Feints.

Use Box effects to deal automatic damage while keeping enough resources to defend yourself.

Teleport and Hera allow you to maintain pressure without giving the Raptors easy counterplay.

Kill one Raptor as quickly as possible — once one is gone, their overall pressure drops dramatically.

Bruce Lee: Bruce likes defending with 3-value cards, while Pandora can attack over them with boosted 7s and 8s.

His 3-value attacks are also inefficient against Pandora's defensive options and auto-damage.

Be especially careful about early Skirmishes — they are one of the few cards that can significantly disrupt your plan.

Keep attacking and use healing to win the damage race.

Houdini: Pandora can comfortably outpace Houdini, even when he starts the game.

Houdini does not like defending consistently, which gives Pandora room to apply repeated pressure.

Use healing to offset the damage taken while maintaining aggression.

Keep attacking rather than allowing Houdini to dictate the pace of the game.

Nicola Tesla: Do not play this matchup slowly — Tesla must be pressured constantly.

Attack frequently and force him to spend his coils defensively.

Use Teleport to prevent Tesla from simply escaping your pressure.

Pandora's aggression can overwhelm Tesla before he gets enough value from his engine.

Little Red Riding Hood: Little Red Riding Hood struggles to withstand Pandora's strong attacks and healing.

Use your healing effects to negate her automatic damage whenever possible.

When Huntsman card does not have the relevant symbol, use Looking Back to reliably return Hera and continue the pressure.

John Henry: John Henry has no reliable Feint, which makes him very vulnerable to Pandora's attacks.

Pressure him directly rather than giving him time to establish his gameplan.

Pandora can simply overwhelm him with repeated attacks and mobility.

Rosie the Riveter: Wait for Rosie's upgrades to fall off before committing to your strongest attacks.

Once her defense drops to 2 or lower, Hera becomes extremely effective.

Use one or two strong attacks at the right moment to finish her quickly.

Tomoe Gozen: This matchup becomes favorable when Pandora gets a strong Box opening.

Use Teleport and your high-value attacks to punish Tomoe when she cannot maintain distance.

If Tomoe retreats with Tis'o, do not chase recklessly — open the Box, Teleport and use Hera or a boosted maneuver to reset the situation.

Do not give her an easy opportunity to control the pace of the game.

Jill Trent: Pandora's aggression is particularly effective against Jill Trent's slow value game.

Do not allow Jill to spend the game freely maneuvering and building value.

Use Teleport and repeated attacks to keep her under constant pressure.

You need to manage your own damage carefully — use healing and know when to disengage.

Golden Bat: Golden Bat does not like being attacked repeatedly, so keep the pressure constant.

Use Teleport or boosted maneuvers to stay on top of him and prevent him from simply running away.

Map choice matters a lot here — Pandora wants a map that allows her to maintain contact.

Do not let Golden Bat turn the matchup into a slow game where he can freely reposition.
