Chupacabra: Unmatched hero guide

Chupacabra is an Unmatched hero from Battle of Legends, Volume Three. Elo rating 1497, ranked #43 of 74 heroes. Playstyle: Aggro / Value. Complexity: 2.5/5.

Strengths

Strong sustain through healing allows winning long trades

Solid defensive value with 3-value cards against common 4 attacks

Can apply constant pressure without fearing damage trades

Capable of explosive double-combo finishes with high damage output

Flexible gameplan: can play aggressive or slow value depending on matchup

Weaknesses

Limited maximum defense values make it vulnerable to big burst turns

Struggles against heavy control, feints, and card denial

Relies on landing value trades — bad exchanges can snowball quickly

Hard matchups against fighters with strong high-value defenses or constant feints

General tips

Can apply constant pressure without fearing damage trades

Can also play slow thanks to healing and high mobility (move 3)

Flexible pacing depending on matchup and game state

If you play aggressively, force constant trades — your healing gives you the edge

Use 3-value defenses efficiently against 4 attacks to gain value

Do not be afraid to take damage — you can recover it later

Look for opportunities to transition into a finishing combo

Against slower decks, pressure early before they stabilize

Manage your hand carefully — you still need resources for your finishing turn

Most games are won through pressure + final burst, not pure aggression

Matchup tips

Winter Soldier: Chupacabra can quickly overwhelm Soldier because he does not like dealing with 4-value attacks.

Keep the pressure on and force him to spend his limited defenses.

Do not give him time to stabilize.

Nikola Tesla: Tesla does not like being attacked repeatedly, which plays directly into Chupacabra's gameplan.

Keep attacking and use your healing to recover from the damage you take.

Do not allow Tesla enough time to build value with his coils.

Robin Hood: Robin Hood has only 3-value defenses and lacks reliable defensive retreats.

Keep attacking — Chupacabra's 4-value attacks put constant pressure on his defenses.

Use your healing to comfortably survive the trades.

Raptors: The Raptors have no card draw, making them vulnerable to Chupacabra's sustained pressure.

Keep attacking and force them to spend their limited hand.

Your healing gives you an advantage in the damage race.

Little Red Riding Hood: Use Ambush to punish her symbols and force her into bad defensive choices.

Keep the pressure constant — she has a hard time surviving repeated attacks.

Yennefer & Triss: Yennefer is a serious opponent because she can choose to attack Chupacabra instead of playing passively.

The goal is to eat through her defenses faster than she can pressure you.

She has fewer defenses than Triss, making her much more vulnerable to Chupacabra's aggression.

The Wayward Sisters: This matchup was initially considered even, but testing showed an advantage for Chupacabra.

Keep pressuring them and use your healing to win the long damage race.

Do not give them enough time to establish their own value engine.

King Arthur: Arthur does not have enough defense to comfortably withstand Chupacabra's pressure.

Keep attacking and force him into repeated defensive trades.

Your healing allows you to stay aggressive without being afraid of the damage race.

Michelangelo: Michelangelo does not have enough defenses to stop Chupacabra's pressure.

Keep attacking and force him to spend his defensive cards.

Do not give him time to build his multi-action turns.

Annie Christmas: This matchup is slightly unfavorable and requires careful aggression.

Annie can bring Chupacabra down to 6 HP and finish her with boosts if you play carelessly.

Use healing to prevent her from converting early damage into a lethal turn.

Rosie the Riveter: This matchup is heavily favored for Chupacabra.

Rosie mainly wants to attack, which allows Chupacabra to absorb the pressure with 3-value defenses.

Use your 3-value defenses against her 4-value attacks and heal back the damage you take.

Once Rosie's upgrades fall off, unleash a combo of 4-value attacks to finish her.

Wyatt Earp: Wyatt is another matchup where Chupacabra's 3-value defenses are extremely effective against repeated 4-value attacks.

Let him spend his attacks while you preserve your health through healing.

Golden Bat: The matchup depends heavily on the map.

Center 5 favors Golden Bat, while Center 4 is more favorable for Chupacabra.

Avoid Yukon — the map makes the matchup significantly worse.

Moon Knight: This is a fast and aggressive matchup where the key question is who kills the other first.

Play aggressively and do not allow Knight to establish a comfortable defensive position.

Chupacabra can use her healing to survive the early exchanges and then finish the fight with a burst.

T-Rex: This is essentially an all-in aggression matchup.

Both fighters want to race each other down before the opponent can stabilize.

From first position, Chupacabra can commit to an all-in Full Send approach.

Titania: The matchup is even and depends heavily on the map.

On a large map, Titania can stall and put Chupacabra to sleep.

On a small map, Chupacabra can close the distance and overwhelm her.

Geralt of Rivia: The matchup is about who can establish pressure first.

Use 3-value defenses against Geralt's swords and keep Feints for Tinker Bell.

Try to force Geralt to use his strong defenses defensively — his other defensive options are much weaker.

Your 0-value cards work very well against his 4s and 5s.

Be careful around his potion, which can make Ambush less effective.

Pandora: This is an even matchup.

Chupacabra can either play for value and healing or switch into an aggressive gameplan.

Pandora's performance depends on her Box draws, while Chupacabra's success depends on reading the interactions correctly.

Be flexible and choose between slow value and direct pressure based on the game state.

Alice: This is an even matchup.

Alice's sidekick can deal significant damage, but Chupacabra can answer with equally strong attacks.

Be especially careful around Alice's Mirrors and try to Feint them whenever possible.

Do not give her easy value from her defensive tools.

Tomoe Gozen: Tomoe can spend the game running away, making the matchup difficult to approach directly.

A straightforward aggression plan does not work well because her defensive game is stronger.

Try to read her hand and commit to attacks when you expect her to hold red and yellow cards.

You need to take calculated risks rather than simply chasing her around the map.

Leonardo: Leonardo's automatic damage can actually help Chupacabra reach the low-health range needed for her finishing turn.

His defenses are not strong enough to comfortably withstand Chupacabra's pressure.

Use the damage he gives you as an opportunity to set up your own burst.

Raphael: Rafael is heavily pressured by Chupacabra's aggression.

His Relentless is only a 3, so Chupacabra's 3-value defenses can handle it efficiently.

Watch out for his Payback effects, which can become 5s.

Bruce Lee: This is a pure all-in brawl where both fighters want to kill the other first.

Commit to aggression and do not let Bruce establish a comfortable tempo.

The matchup is decided primarily by the damage race and the timing of each fighter's burst.

John Henry: This matchup is better for Chupacabra than expected.

John Henry's lack of reliable Feints makes him vulnerable to Chupacabra's attacks.

Use your superior card draw to maintain pressure and keep attacking.

Do not be afraid to pressure him even when he reaches the health threshold where his hammers become dangerous.

Sinbad: This is a pure Full Send matchup.

Chupacabra attacks Sinbad while Sinbad attacks back.

Commit to the damage race and try to finish him before his stronger late-game tools take over.

Bigfoot: This is a very unfavorable matchup for Chupacabra.

Bigfoot has strong 4 and 5 defenses, multiple Feints and 6-value attacks.

His automatic damage and strong mobility make it difficult for Chupacabra to establish her usual pressure.

Be especially careful about the Rabbit, which is difficult to remove.

Jill Trent: Jill Trent is favored because her healing works extremely well against Chupacabra's low defensive values.

Her 5-value attacks are difficult for Chupacabra's printed 3-value defenses to handle efficiently.

There are two possible plans: full-send Jill while ignoring Daisy, or kill Daisy first and switch to a value game.

Achilles: Achilles is a difficult matchup for Chupacabra.

He can pressure Chupacabra with strong 4-value attacks and punish her directly.

Even keeping Patroclus alive, Achilles can eventually overwhelm Chupacabra with his attacks and Feints.

Yennenga: Yennenga does not have many Feints, which gives Chupacabra opportunities to land attacks.

However, she has many defensive retreats and a large health pool.

Do not expect a quick kill — you need to steadily work through her defenses.

Daredevil: Daredevil has an enormous number of Feints, making it extremely difficult for Chupacabra to execute her normal attack plan.

He can effectively deny the attacks and interactions Chupacabra relies on.

Do not expect to win through a straightforward aggression plan.

Dr. Sattler: Sattler's defenses are particularly bad for Chupacabra because her 0-value cards lose much of their effectiveness.

She can pressure Chupacabra heavily with her attacks.

Avoid relying on your 0-value defensive interactions and look for other ways to absorb her pressure.

The Genie: Genie's value game works very well against Chupacabra.

He can discard Chupacabra's best cards and either keep running away or finish her with his accumulated value.

Do not allow Genie to dictate the pace of the game through repeated retreats and value generation.

Leshen: Leshen can simply avoid Chupacabra for most of the game.

His ability to stall and pass makes it very difficult for Chupacabra to reach him.

He can repeatedly heal back damage and maintain a high health total.

If Leshen makes a mistake and allows Chupacabra to reach him at low health, she can still finish him with a combo.

Houdini: Houdini can discard Chupacabra's only defense, making the matchup more dangerous.

Despite that, Chupacabra can still play the matchup through aggression.

Going first is particularly valuable because it lets Chupacabra establish pressure before Houdini gets comfortable.

The matchup is generally easier for Houdini, but Chupacabra can absolutely win it.

Ciri: Ciri is another matchup where Chupacabra needs to rely on aggression.

Keep pressure on her before she can establish her own tempo and value.

Use healing to survive the early exchanges and look for the finishing window.
