Loki: Unmatched hero guide

Loki is an Unmatched hero from Battle of Legends, Volume Three. Elo rating 1550, ranked #39 of 74 heroes. Playstyle: Aggro / Tempo / Mind Games. Complexity: 3.5/5.

Strengths

Unique ability revolves around giving Tricks to the opponent and punishing them for playing or discarding them

Extremely strong card draw allows constant pressure and aggressive play

Tricks increase movement, making it difficult for the opponent to escape

Strong attacks and solid defenses, especially against fighters with high card values

Can manipulate the opponent's hand and force specific cards to be played

Powerful recursion allows important Tricks to be returned to hand or placed on top of the deck

Weaknesses

Extremely demanding to play and heavily punishes mistakes

Has no real value or exhaustion gameplan — must kill the opponent before running out of cards

Giving Tricks to the opponent can backfire against fighters who benefit from receiving strong cards

Struggles heavily when the opponent keeps only 3–4 cards in hand

Very dependent on finding the red 4 Tricks early; having them near the bottom of the deck can lose the game

Limited against fighters with low card values or many cards that change their values

Has no true passes, making it difficult to slow the game down

General tips

Keep the opponent's hand large — your ability becomes much stronger when they hold several Tricks

Play aggressively and keep attacking; you do not have a reliable value or exhaustion plan

Find the red 4 Tricks as early as possible — their automatic damage is a major part of your win condition

Use your Schemes to pull the opponent back into range and set up Ragnarok turns

Ragnarok is usually a 3–6 attack, not the huge attack its ability suggests; use it when the opponent has 2–3 Tricks in hand

Use your Tricks as bait — cards like Werewolf can force high defenses or Feints and create another attack

Freya's Sanctuary and other Tricks can be used offensively, not only as defenses

Use Looking for Trouble to see the opponent's hand and force them to play the card you want

Use Leviathan to reuse important Tricks, manipulate the opponent's hand, and punish high-value cards

Be careful not to overdraw — Loki can run out of cards surprisingly quickly

Track the opponent's hand size constantly; keeping them at 3–4 cards makes finding damage much harder

Your goal is to accumulate small chunks of damage from Tricks and convert them into a final kill

Matchup tips

Bigfoot: Bigfoot's high card values and boosts give Loki strong defensive targets.

Use his high-value cards against him and take advantage of your strong defenses.

Keep Tricks in his hand to increase your movement and maintain pressure.

Achilles: Achilles can benefit heavily from receiving your Tricks, so be careful about which ones you give him.

Use his Helmets against him when possible, but watch out for accidentally giving Patroclus value.

Leviathan can help manipulate his hand and reuse important Tricks.

Alice: Alice's strong boosts give Leviathan excellent targets to copy.

Use her high-value cards to amplify your own attacks when possible.

Be careful about giving her Tricks that provide too much value.

Little Red Riding Hood: Looking for Trouble is especially useful for manipulating Hood's hand.

Use it to see what she has and force her to play the card you want.

Be careful with her Symbols and avoid giving her the exact cards she needs.

Electra: Looking for Trouble can reveal Electra's hand and let you manipulate her options.

Use your Tricks to control which cards she is forced to play.

Be aware of her ability to replace cards with her sidekick.

Raphael: Rafael's boosts are particularly effective against Loki's variable-value defenses.

Avoid relying on your changing-value defenses to stop his biggest attacks.

Try to maintain distance and use Tricks to control his hand.

Moon Knight: Moon Knight's variable-value attacks can reduce the effectiveness of Loki's defenses.

Be careful about giving him Tricks that he can convert into additional value.

Use your movement advantage to keep pressure on him.
