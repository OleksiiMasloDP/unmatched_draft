Sun Wukong: Unmatched hero guide

Sun Wukong is an Unmatched hero from Battle of Legends, Volume Two. Elo rating 1910, ranked #8 of 74 heroes. Playstyle: Value / Wall / Combo. Complexity: 3.5/5.

Strengths

His ability lets him summon a clone every turn (at the cost of 1 HP to himself or the clone), building a wall that simply shuts down most melee fighters regardless of how hard they hit — they can't get through to him at all.

Keeps attacking relentlessly to bait out one or two of the opponent's cards while he plays his own patient value game.

Can heal through Phoenix Form, bottom unwanted cards from the opponent's hand, and his sidekicks can play any card in his deck.

A key attack card whose effect cannot be cancelled, making it excellent value even on token-based maps.

Weaknesses

No scheme, feint, or cancel effects at all — characters built around repeated maneuvering and disengaging (e.g. Winter Soldier) can just outplay him turn after turn with nothing he can do about it.

No card draw, so aggressive ranged fighters can still pressure and outrace him.

Depends on drawing his cards in matchups where he actually has to play the real game instead of just walling up with clones.

General tips

Against most melee fighters, just build the clone wall and let the matchup play itself out — they simply can't get through.

Against ranged/maneuver-heavy opponents who ignore the clones, you have to actually play the value game: bait with your attacks, hold your best defenses for the moments that matter, and keep your 72 transformation engine running.

Guard your uncancellable card and your redirect-damage defense carefully — they're your biggest value swings and your best answer to boosted burst damage.

Sustain matters a lot — treat your heal/pass card as one of your two or three most important cards in almost every matchup, since it both heals and buys you tempo.

Know which matchups need you to play actively and aggressively instead of passively walling up — several 'even' matchups are only even if you actually pressure back rather than just hiding behind clones.

Matchup tips

Bigfoot: An easy matchup — he dumps his big attacks into your clones and has no real way to get through the wall.

Even if he does reach you, throw your 0 or your redirect-damage defense, retreat, and rebuild the wall.

Tomoe Gozen: She has no answer to the clone wall.

Your 7s land especially well here specifically because of the clones.

Donatello: Correct positioning and walling up with clones is everything — many of his attacks simply get lost in them.

Michelangelo: Easy in practice, even though he theoretically has a shot if you misplay — he mostly just breaks his extra actions on your clones.

Robert Muldoon: Favorable but not trivial — as a ranged fighter he can try to shoot you down, pressure you, and win on value if you're not careful.

Luke Cage: Favors you, but he can bait a couple of guesses with his maneuvers — misread those and you can fail to close him out and lose on value instead.

Golden Bat: Easier for you overall, though a skilled Golden Bat on a large map can maneuver around and win on discards/value — still, the matchup is more comfortable on your side.

Since you have no cancel effects, he can discard your cards fairly freely — a lot hinges on which cards he discards and what he shuffles back into his deck.

Bullseye: Look for a map with a range-5 center for a more comfortable game.

Expect him to pressure hard and try to bait out your defenses.

Leshen: Play actively — a passive game just lets Leshen outlast you.

Getting rid of his wolves matters, but don't spend a 7 on them.

Use your extra-action turns to pressure him into using up his defenses, then finish him off with a 7 — hold that combo and plan for it.

Use your redirect-damage defense against his +3 attack.

Black Panther: Very map-dependent and somewhat coin-flippy — take the map center early and it's largely decided, though he can still try to pressure.

Jill Trent: Trickier than most favorable matchups — it comes down to how fast you kill her sidekick and how much value she banks from her schemes before that happens.

Titania: Favorable overall, but a skilled Titania player can still win through a well-timed combo.

Shredder: Roughly favorable for you, but if he catches you without a good ultimate defense in hand, his big multi-action combo can shred through you fast — always keep one in reserve.

Pandora: Winnable, but she needs good luck from her ability box (particularly a boost) and reasonable defense cards to compete — otherwise your pressure is too much for her.

Robin Hood: A genuine even matchup about healing timing and positioning — draw Phoenix Form early and you survive comfortably; go without it too long, especially on a tight-centered map, and he can grind you down.

Little Red Riding Hood: Since this is an uphill fight, don't rely on stalling behind a clone wall — a long grind favors her healing. Spread your clones across separate cells rather than stacking them, since her attack and mobility let her pick through them regardless.

Look to commit your 7s and finish the game early rather than waiting for a perfect setup.

Her uncancellable attack can endlessly chip away at your clones.

She has movement effects that let her close the distance to you quickly.

Ghost Rider: Usually best played without leaning on clones at all — chase him down and race him with your big attacks, since he'll flatten stacked clones in one maneuver otherwise.

If you do use clones, spread them out so he can't wipe more than one per maneuver. His only free defenses are limited — most of his real defense costs him Hellfire.

Sherlock Holmes: A real interaction matchup — he has to actually play against you or you'll simply beat him down, and you have to actually play against him or his scheme engine will strangle you.

Harry Houdini: Swings heavily on who goes first. Assuming he goes first (the harder version of this matchup for you), pick tight, closeable maps rather than open ones — he can otherwise deal a lot of damage before your wall is up.

If he goes second instead, the matchup becomes strongly in your favor.

The Genie: An aggressive ranged fighter who can pressure you through the clone tax — a genuinely even fight where you're trying to avoid getting caught by his big combo turn while grinding him down slowly.

Black Widow: Depends heavily on whether The Kinshasa Directive lands for her — if it does, she can discard your important cards or a large chunk of your hand and grind out the game comfortably; play positionally and carefully either way.

She-Hulk: A genuinely nasty, heavily random matchup because of one of her cards, which can hand you exactly what you need or exactly nothing — completely out of your control either way.

Her other big effects are unpleasant too, but you do have tools against them: your ultimate defenses answer her strongest attacks, and your ignore-effect attacks force extra discards. Winnable on value, but honestly not a fun matchup either way.

Blackbeard: A genuinely interesting matchup built around his doubloon-fueled discards versus your clone positioning — whether he can accumulate enough doubloons to strip your key cards before you overwhelm him with clones and pressure.

Ciri: Surprisingly even given she's melee, thanks to her huge amount of auto-damage and random hand discards that chip you down regardless of clones.

Use your hand-peek scheme to bottom her key returnable cards — it meaningfully cuts into her recursion.

Yennefer & Triss: A very strong value hero — if she gets the initiative, her maneuver-echo-boost loop can grind down almost anyone alone, so you can't just wall up passively here.

Play actively and aggressively: pressure her, force discards and maneuvers, and don't let her sit comfortably and loop her value engine. Slightly favors her overall, but a skilled Wukong can win it.

Yennenga: She has a huge health pool, her point-blank/cone attacks kill clones outright, and her double-attacks strip cards from your hand.

She can comfortably absorb two 7s by redirecting the damage into your clones.

Don't lean on the clone wall as your main plan here — you need to actually pressure and close the game out rather than stall, since her large health pool means a slow grind favors her.

Try to bait out her archers or point-blank tools before you commit your big attacks, rather than throwing them into a fresh board state.

Daredevil: A slow, unpleasant matchup — he mostly just runs from you and periodically lands a card that shuffles several of your cards away, on repeat.

Medusa: Rough matchup — her ability kills clones, and her turtle-into-gaze combo is an instant loss if it lands. Outside of that, you can grind, kite, and bait her down carefully.

Bruce Lee: He has a strong combo including an attack that cancels your redirect-defense and teleport, plus a punch that shreds clones too.

He can also just win through random 4-5 discards backed by a card that returns his cancel attack, so it's not only about the combo.

T-Rex: Wins through combo regardless of your clones, simply trampling through the wall.

Winter Soldier: Repositions, cycles his cards, and slowly grinds you out with nothing you can really answer.

Nikola Tesla: His zone auto-damage, remote movement control, and ignore-value attacks are all very strong against you.

You want his damage effects landing on your 0/ignore defense specifically (it's a flat 2 damage no matter how much he boosts) — you don't want his discard/extra-action/cancel effects landing on your redirect defense or your 0.

Elektra: She just runs from you — you won't be able to do much to her.

Doctor Strange: He discards and ignores your attack cards.

He heals by dumping value into your clones with his 4.

Avoid feeding him easy value by attacking predictably — vary your approach and try to force him to spend cards he doesn't want to.
