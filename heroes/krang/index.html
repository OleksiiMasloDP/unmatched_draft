Krang: Unmatched hero guide

Krang is an Unmatched hero from Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. Elo rating 1614, ranked #32 of 74 heroes. Playstyle: Aggro / Combo / Value. Complexity: 4/5.

Strengths

Very high burst damage through boosted attacks (6–8)

Strong card draw and healing engine via IQ

Flexible gameplans: aggression, slowdown, or fatigue win

Good mobility with schemes and movement effects

Powerful tempo control with turn-ending effects (Minimizer)

Can generate huge value swings with Pan Dimension Portal

Weaknesses

Unstable outcomes due to heavy reliance on dice rolls

Weak and inconsistent defenses

Key damage cards often needed for attack, not defense

Can lose games from bad Portal or IQ rolls

Vulnerable if forced to waste high-value cards defensively

General tips

Mulligan aggressively for IQ — it is your core card

Play proactively: pressure with big attacks and tempo tools

Do not waste Molecular Amplification Unit on defense unless necessary

Use Minimizer to deny opponent turns and regain tempo

Portal can reset hand advantage — use it when behind on cards

You can switch to slow play and win via fatigue if needed

Manage hand carefully before committing to big combo turns

Accept taking damage early if you can heal it back with IQ

Primary plan: overwhelm opponent with constant pressure

Alternative plan: stall, heal, and win through exhaustion

Key cards

  • Iq
  • Pan Dimension Portal
  • Molecular Amplification Unit
  • Minimizer

Matchup tips

Houdini: Your discard effects are extremely strong against Houdini.

Use end-turn effects to deny him the value he wants from his cards.

Keep the pressure high — Houdini struggles to generate enough value against Krang.

Little Red Riding Hood: Hood has very limited ways to meaningfully threaten Krang.

Use your superior card value and pressure to keep her from stabilizing.

Do not give her time to build a strong hand and establish her gameplan.

Your discard effects are especially effective against Huntsman's low-value defenses.

Exploit the lack of Feints to make your attacks more reliable.

Be careful around his 6-value attacks, but do not let them stop you from applying pressure.

Use IQ to sustain yourself while continuing to pressure him.
