Blackbeard: Unmatched hero guide

Blackbeard is an Unmatched hero from Battle of Legends, Volume Three. Elo rating 1935, ranked #6 of 74 heroes. Playstyle: Aggro / Value / Hand Control. Complexity: 4/5.

Strengths

Extremely flexible ability allows switching between aggressive pressure and slow value play

Dubloons enable powerful extra-action turns and are the core resource of the character

Strong hand disruption can remove key cards from the opponent before they can be used

Excellent value from card effects when the opponent cannot pay the ransom

Strong ranged pressure combined with two disposable sidekicks

Can generate multiple attacks and movement actions in a single turn

Healing and sidekick recursion provide strong sustain in longer games

Weaknesses

Very few defensive cards make him vulnerable to sustained aggression

Loses a large part of his gameplan if the opponent can consistently steal Dubloons

Limited card draw makes running out of cards a serious threat

Strong ranged fighters can pressure him while denying access to Dubloons

His strongest effects become much less reliable when the opponent has enough Dubloons to pay for them

Can struggle against fighters who can repeatedly force him to spend cards defensively

General tips

Track Dubloons constantly — your entire gameplan changes depending on who controls them

Use your extra action when it creates real value: double attacks, attack plus movement, or closing the game

Do not underestimate your third action — it can generate cards, force defenses, chase fighters, or finish sidekicks

Use Plunder to take the opponent's last Dubloon without interacting with them

Fearsome and Calculating is your most important card — use it when the opponent has no Dubloons

Use hand disruption to remove the opponent's key cards before committing to your strongest attacks

Do not always play aggressively — against fighters who cannot reach or steal your Dubloons, slow value play can be much stronger

Keep your sidekicks relevant and use them to create pressure, block movement, or enable strong attacks

When you have the resources, look for turns where an extra action converts directly into multiple attacks

Against aggressive fighters, use your defensive cards carefully — you cannot afford to spend them unnecessarily

Your goal is not simply to collect Dubloons, but to turn them into tempo, card advantage, and pressure

If the opponent cannot consistently take your Dubloons, Blackbeard can overwhelm them through superior value

Matchup tips

Ciri: Ciri struggles to withstand Blackbeard's pressure and hand disruption.

She has a hard time taking Dubloons, which leaves Blackbeard free to generate value.

Use your pressure and discard effects to keep her from setting up her gameplan.

Donatello: Donatello struggles to take Dubloons and has difficulty reaching Blackbeard.

Use your ranged pressure and hand disruption to keep him from setting up his combo.

Do not give him time to build his resources and prepare a big combo turn.

Yennenga: Yennenga struggles to take Dubloons and cannot easily reach Blackbeard.

Use your ranged pressure and hand disruption to keep her under pressure.

Your strong defenses make it difficult for her to convert her attacks into Dubloon steals.

Little Red Riding Hood: Blackbeard can disrupt Hood's Symbols very effectively.

Use your discard effects to remove the cards she needs before she can convert them into value.

Keep pressure on her so she cannot comfortably set up her Symbols.

Medusa: This is a relatively even matchup where both fighters can generate value through discard.

Dubloons are less reliable here, so the game becomes a battle over whose important cards get discarded.

Do not rely entirely on your Dubloon advantage — focus on disrupting her key cards.

Sherlock Holmes: Sherlock cannot comfortably play his usual passive game because Blackbeard's pressure will overwhelm him.

Force him to interact instead of allowing him to sit back and generate value.

Use your pressure and hand disruption to make him spend his resources.

Robin Hood: Robin Hood is dangerous because he can steal Dubloons and apply constant ranged pressure.

You have limited card draw, so be prepared to spend many cards defending yourself.

Do not allow him to freely control the distance and repeatedly take your Dubloons.

Bigfoot: Bigfoot can hit Blackbeard extremely hard and punish the lack of defenses.

Do not underestimate his damage output even if the matchup is statistically favorable.

Use your ranged pressure and hand disruption to prevent him from getting comfortable.

Achilles: Achilles can punish Blackbeard heavily through his strong attacks.

Your lack of defenses makes getting pressured by Achilles particularly dangerous.

Try to keep him at range and disrupt his important cards before he can establish pressure.

Luke Cage: Luke can break through Blackbeard's defenses with his strong attacks.

Avoid letting him establish sustained pressure.

Use ranged attacks and hand disruption to control his resources before committing to a close fight.

Bullseye: This is primarily an aggression matchup.

Marked can win confidently if he gets his Schemes at the right time.

Use your discard effects to remove his Schemes — without them, his gameplan falls apart.

She-Hulk: The matchup is highly random and depends heavily on the interactions you hit.

Blackbeard has good defensive cards against many of Hulk's effects, but Lady Justice is a major problem.

Try to predict and defend Hulk's high-value attacks while using your pistols to punish Lady Justice.

Raphael: This is a volatile matchup that depends heavily on the map.

Discarding Unbridled or Relentless is particularly valuable.

On Raphael's map he can overwhelm Blackbeard with mobility and pressure.

Jill Trent: Jill has a lot of value and strong defenses.

The matchup depends heavily on how quickly you can eliminate Daisy.

Remove Daisy early to make the rest of the matchup much more manageable.

Doctor Strange: The matchup contains a lot of randomness because of Strange's Fog interactions.

Blackbeard has enough cards to punish Strange's healing and keep his health under pressure.

Force Strange to spend health and resources rather than allowing him to play his usual passive game.

Map choice can change the matchup significantly.
