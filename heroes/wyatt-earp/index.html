Wyatt Earp: Unmatched hero guide

Wyatt Earp is an Unmatched hero from Stars & Stripes. Elo rating 1555, ranked #38 of 74 heroes. Playstyle: Tempo / Aggro. Complexity: 2.5/5.

Strengths

Excellent at controlling the game tempo

High depth with lots of micro-decisions & 3 distinct abilities

Thrives in aggro & exhaustion setups (heavy pressure + 4 scheme passes)

Rewarding mind games and highly tactical 50-50 / 40-60 matchups

Blind-pickable fighter relying heavily on solid fundamentals

Weaknesses

Losing Doc Holliday early drastically reduces map control and offensive options

Struggles against exceptionally tanky fighters, heavy card draw, or hard attack punishers

General tips

Offense is your best defense. All 3 parts of your ability are very good

Think when to boost and full-aggro, and when to play for tempo and value

Take more risks when you are behind. Two ranged fighters give crazy map control

You can run most fighters out of defenses. Mulligan for 'You're A Daisy if You Do'

Find creative spots for movement schemes and Doc reds. Throw bait attacks, boost away your blue cards

Keep track of their bottom-decked cards. Think when to remove damage or defense

Look for ways to empty your hand for 'In Vino Veritas', but don't be afraid to play it to draw 1-2 cards or as a pass

Great 'filler' fighter for your pool. Impactful decision-making every game. When in doubt, full send!
