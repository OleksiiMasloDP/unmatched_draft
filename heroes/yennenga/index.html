Yennenga: Unmatched hero guide

Yennenga is an Unmatched hero from Battle of Legends, Volume Two. Elo rating 1822, ranked #15 of 74 heroes. Playstyle: Value / Tempo / Aggro / Combo. Complexity: 3.5/5.

Strengths

Extreme flexibility: can easily switch between standard slow value, aggressive tempo, pure aggro, or combo setups

Incredible survivability using good base defense and unique mechanics to disperse damage onto respawnable 2-HP archer sidekicks

High skill expression that rewards strong game fundamentals and deep matchup spread knowledge

Potential to gain massive action advantage and even burst down certain fighters in a single turn

Weaknesses

Suffers heavily against hyper-value fighters who can continuously cheat infinite value into play

Lacks direct cancel effects, making her highly vulnerable to fighters with exceptionally powerful card effects

Requires precise positioning and tricky archer management; grouping them leaves them open to dangerous area-of-effect damage

General tips

Recognize your win condition early: know when to poke for a combo, when to control tempo, and when to rush the enemy down

Master archer deployment: spread them out to control map space and avoid enemy boost combos, or body-block to shield Yennenga

Carefully balance your choices between respawning archers for defense or taking action gains for aggressive tempo swings

Synergize movement effects to setup point-blank shots, land high-value Momentum Shifts, or trap enemies into Jaws of the Beast

Use clever baits and mix-ups to control the board, minimize your own boosts, and abuse auto-damage when pressured
