Tomoe Gozen: Unmatched hero guide

Tomoe Gozen is an Unmatched hero from Sun's Origin. Elo rating 1718, ranked #25 of 74 heroes. Playstyle: Aggro / Value / Hero-Hunter. Complexity: 4/5.

Strengths

A true hero-hunter — her deck is built around chasing down and killing enemy heroes, with plenty of strong defenses that block big hits, force discards, or reposition the opponent.

Can bank a large effective health pool (up to 20 with her scheme's heal) since she'll be taking a lot of hits from both the hero and any sidekicks.

Huge variety of tools gives her genuine gameplan flexibility rather than a single all-in line — tempo through card draw, big flat value, mobility, or a setup-into-big-attack combo.

Two 7-value attack cards in the deck is rare and very strong when you can land them.

Weaknesses

Struggles hard against sidekicks — her pseudo-cancel effect only works on heroes, and she has few real resources to kill sidekicks outright, so opponent sidekicks reliably land hits on her.

Move 2 with no sidekick of her own means poor board control on her own.

Very positioning-dependent — she needs contained, clearly-cut map zones to actually trigger her ability, and tends to want to play on maps that favor her, which is a real limitation in tournament formats with map bans/vetoes.

General tips

Prefer maps with smaller, cleanly-cut zones and a reachable range-2 center — these let her ability trigger consistently. Avoid very large maps, oversized zones, or maps where doors/passages constantly shuffle zone membership.

Don't be afraid to spend one of your 7s on a genuinely problematic solo sidekick — losing that resource is often worth it if it lets you play the rest of the fight one-on-one against the hero.

Set up your biggest attack rather than throwing it blind — bait a weaker defense out first (with a piercing/tempo attack) before committing your best hit into a fresh hand of defenses.

Her draw/discard tool is strong but situational — often safer to play it in defense and cash in the profit on your own turn rather than force it into an attack.

Matchup tips

Dracula: Favorable — he simply lacks the damage output to kill Tomoe.

Alice: Favorable for the same reason — not enough damage output to threaten her.

Ghost Rider: Favorable — she'll typically kill him faster than he can run her over.

Moon Knight: A very good matchup for her — his ability can't pierce her defenses well, while she pierces his easily.

Daredevil: Favorable — he can't withstand her tempo.

Winter Soldier: Same story as Daredevil — can't keep up with her tempo.

Bullseye: Favorable — he simply can't pierce her defenses.

Black Panther: Debatable but ultimately favorable for Tomoe — her two 7s and value pressure can force him to over-boost and lose from there.

Black Widow: Favorable — she has too much auto-damage and her 7s for him to keep up with.

Jill Trent: Favorable — use her scheme's move-through-sidekick option to ignore Daisy entirely and dump everything straight into Jill.

Golden Bat: Favorable — he likes to run away, which plays directly into her ability's punish for leaving her zone.

Nikola Tesla: Genuinely favorable for Tomoe despite the common perception otherwise — he has to guess interactions constantly and spend coils on suboptimal cards; one coil-spending mistake and her 7 lands cleanly.

She also simply out-draws him (around 5 cards of draw available to her deck vs his roughly 3).

Bruce Lee: Favorable — he lacks the damage to threaten her, so kill him quickly.

Oda Nobunaga: Considered favorable, though only played once — throw a 7 into his sidekick early and he has little left to work with.

The Wayward Sisters: Very favorable — they simply don't have the value or damage output to threaten her.

Titania: Favorable — she lacks value and damage output against Tomoe.

Hamlet: Favorable — he tends to beat himself up more than Tomoe needs to.

Sinbad: Favorable — kill his sidekick quickly, and she has enough defense and damage to handle Sinbad himself.

She-Hulk: A genuinely even matchup — arguably a slight lean toward Tomoe until the very last card, but close either way.

Raptors: Even and genuinely dangerous — they can bite hard if you're not careful.

Robin Hood: Even — kill his Merry Men quickly with her 4-value cards and he has no real damage left; let them set up their combo and he can pressure hard instead.

William Shakespeare: Even — both sides deal a lot of damage, so it comes down to who kills first.

Luke Cage: Even — the race is whether Cage/Misty kill Tomoe first, or she kills Misty first; once Misty is dead, Cage alone usually lacks enough damage to finish her.

Bigfoot: Even in theory but two well-placed 7s can put him away.

Harry Houdini: Even, but she needs to kill Bess (his ranged sidekick with a strong 6) quickly — if Bess survives, the matchup shifts in Houdini's favor.

Annie Christmas: Roughly even, and depends heavily on how fast her sidekick Charlie dies — quickly favors Tomoe, if he survives long it favors Annie.

Achilles: Unfavorable — his sidekick creates a constant cycle she struggles to answer, and it grinds down her value over the game.

Yennenga: Unfavorable — her archers constantly do damage and reposition, and Tomoe has little way to answer that.

Medusa: Unfavorable — expect to be constantly forced into discards over the course of the game.

Robert Muldoon: Unfavorable — she simply can't reliably reach him.

Doctor Strange: Considered a tough matchup requiring aggressive, well-informed play from Tomoe's side rather than her usual patient game.

Little Red Riding Hood: Unfavorable — lots of auto-damage, random discards, and her Huntsman hits for a nasty 6.

The Genie: Unfavorable — he can simply wait for his 7, stockpile wishes, draw up to six cards, and combo you; he also has a lot of discard tools and wins on value.

Sherlock Holmes: Unfavorable — there's very little she can do to answer his gameplan here.

Sun Wukong: Considered one of her worse matchups for that reason.

Elektra: Unfavorable — there's very little Tomoe can offer here.

T-Rex: Unfavorable — same story, not much Tomoe can do about it.
